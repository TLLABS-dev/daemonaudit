## Answers to open questions
- **Partial coverage as a fifth status?** Yes: `incomplete`, and findings are never discarded — a check with findings *and* coverage gaps is `fail` with the gaps in `note`. (`off` was added at the same time so opt-outs don't count as incomplete.)
- **Exit-code precedence with HIGH findings and an errored check?** 2. A HIGH is actionable now regardless; incompleteness is still visible in the table, the summary line (`INCOMPLETE`), and `summary.complete` in JSON. If CI wants to fail on incomplete-even-with-findings it can read the JSON; a `--strict` flag can come later if anyone asks.
- **User-supplied symlink `--home`?** Supported, resolved once; the report shows the resolved path and `meta.home_as_given` keeps the original. Symlinks *inside* the home are never followed.
